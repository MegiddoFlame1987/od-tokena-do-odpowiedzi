How the list is made

These rules keep the list free of AI gossip.

Goes in whenExample
It changes what an ordinary worker can do in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or CopilotA new model on the free plan, a new feature in the chat
It changes a fact quoted in this materialA new price, a new standard, a new generation of models, that is AI programs that write answers
Law, regulation or a security incident that touches companies in the UK or the EUDeadlines in the AI Act, the EU law on AI. A hole in an AI assistant
Never goes in
Company valuations, raising money from investors, board changes, leaks. Announcements with no date. News only for programmers
Confirmed means: read on the page of the maker, a public body or the authors of the study. Reported means: the press described it, but the original source could not be checked. The descriptions are summaries, not quotations. The details are always at the source.
